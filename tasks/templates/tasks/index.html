      ClearStep User Manual

      Neurodivergent Executive Function & Focus Guide

      1. What is ClearStep?

      ClearStep is an AI prosthetic designed to eliminate task paralysis, time blindness, and executive overload. Instead of forcing you to format a rigid checklist, you can ramble chaotic thoughts, and the AI filters your mission, quarantines tangents, and blindsides you with single-step focus.

      2. Energy Battery Calibration

      Before recording, select your current cognitive energy level:

      • LOW (10% Zombie Mode): Generates ultra-tiny physical actions if you are completely drained.
      • STEADY (50%): Standard mode with 5-minute bite-sized steps.
      • HIGH (90% Hyperfocus): Groups actions into power sprint blocks.

      3. Brain Dumps (Voice or Text)

      Click START VOICE BRAIN DUMP and speak freely—include your core goals along with random distractions. Alternatively, type your wall of text into the text box and build your protocol.

      4. Blindfold Mode & Focus Tools

      You will only see one step at a time to prevent overwhelm:

      • 5-Second Starter Trigger: Zero-effort micro-action to begin.
      • Visual Time Horizon Bar: Shifts from green to red to keep track of time.
      • Read Aloud (TTS): Have the browser speak instructions out loud.
      • I'm Stuck (Split Step): Dynamically breaks down an intimidating step into 3 smaller actions.
      • Tangent Parking Lot: Safely traps intrusive thoughts so you don't lose focus.

      5. Zero-Shame Team Handoff
